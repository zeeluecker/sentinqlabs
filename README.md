# sentinqlabs
Sentinq

The Trusted Command Center and Control Plane for AI Agents

Large Language Models are becoming increasingly capable at reasoning.

But reasoning alone is not enough.

As AI agents begin acting on behalf of people—shopping, booking travel, managing calendars, accessing financial services, or coordinating healthcare—we need something they don't naturally provide:

A trusted control plane.

Just as payment tokenization introduced lifecycle management, delegated authority, and secure credential orchestration for payment credentials, Sentinq introduces those same principles for AI agents.

Sentinq becomes the system where people manage:

which agents they trust
what agents know
what agents are allowed to do
which services agents can access
how those permissions evolve over time
the complete lifecycle of every AI relationship

AI models reason.

Sentinq governs.
